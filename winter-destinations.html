Five places to find your winter wonder

Snow-dusted villages, northern lights, and little moments of magic. These five destinations make a case for the colder months.

By Wander editors   ·   November 16, 2026   ·   8 min read

Photo: Unsplash · Illustrative travel photography

In this story01 / Hokkaido, Japan02 / Finnish Lapland03 / The Austrian Alps04 / Tromsø, Norway05 / Banff, Canada

There is something about travelling in winter that makes the small things feel bigger: the first warm drink after a cold walk, the glow of a village window, the silence after fresh snow. These five places offer different ways to find that feeling.

01 / Hokkaido, Japan

For a winter that feels wonderfully far away

In Japan’s northernmost main island, winter sets the pace. Snow softens the landscape, steamy bowls of ramen become an occasion, and an outdoor hot spring can turn an ordinary evening into the highlight of a trip.

Base yourself in Sapporo for city comforts, then make time for smaller towns and open landscapes. You do not need to ski to love the season here; a snowy walk followed by something warm is an itinerary in its own right.

02 / Finnish Lapland

A quieter kind of magic

There is a special hush to a northern forest in winter. In Finnish Lapland, short days invite simple rituals: a walk among snow-covered trees, a sauna, and a long meal shared somewhere cosy.

The northern lights are a possibility rather than a promise. Plan a journey you would enjoy even under cloudy skies, and let any glimpse of green across the darkness become an unexpected extra.

“Winter is an invitation to slow down and see a familiar world differently.”
03 / The Austrian Alps

Village life, with a mountain backdrop

Think wooden chalets, a bakery window, and mountains that make even a short walk feel cinematic. Austria’s Alpine villages reward travellers who leave space between activities.

Choose one base rather than moving every night. Spend a morning exploring on foot, take a scenic train ride when the weather allows, and give yourself permission to linger over a slice of cake.

04 / Tromsø, Norway

Where the winter sky tells the story

Above the Arctic Circle, Tromsø brings together a lively city and extraordinary natural surroundings. Harbour walks, museums, and cafés offer plenty to enjoy between outings into the winter landscape.

Treat guided outdoor trips as weather-dependent additions, not the only reason to visit. Good layers and a flexible schedule will do more for your experience than an overfilled checklist.

05 / Banff, Canada

Big landscapes, small moments

The Canadian Rockies offer a winter landscape of forest, mountain, and sky. Around Banff, even a simple view from town can feel like the opening scene of an adventure.

Winter in the mountains calls for care. Choose maintained routes, check local conditions, and use qualified guides for activities beyond your experience. A slow day with a beautiful view still counts as exploring.

A note for your journey
This is an inspirational editorial guide. Weather, access, and travel requirements can change. Check official local guidance and current conditions when planning your trip.
